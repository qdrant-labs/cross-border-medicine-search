Same query, four retrievers

Over 300 Polish queries: dense+SHIFT 0.827 at top‑1, BM25 0.230, SPLADE 0.770, and RRF fusion of dense with SPLADE 0.837. BM25 loses the way the theory predicts — on 160 of those 300 queries it shares no term at all with any Spanish document, so there is nothing to rank. SPLADE does not lose: term expansion reaches across the vocabulary gap that exact matching cannot, and fusing it with dense beats dense alone at every cut (0.837 / 0.920 / 0.970). That is a real result and it is not what was expected — Splade_PP is English‑trained and running on Spanish and Polish registry strings. The shipped path is still dense + hyperbolic, because +0.010 at top‑1 does not yet pay for a second index and a 500 MB model, but that is a cost argument now, not an accuracy one.
